## Overview 

In this point-and-click cyberpunk physics puzzler, bring the all-seeing Watchers together into sleeper cells to connect and share data. Navigate around obstacles and enemies to create a comprehensive surveillance network. 

## Camera Feature

Sleepwatchers has the option to turn on the user's computer webcam to enhance the game's atmosphere and is for entertainment purposes only. Webcam use is NOT required to play the game. Under no circumstances does the webcam record or stream video or audio footage.
