## Overview 

Slime Solstice was developed for the [Holistic 3D Lockdown Game jam](https://itch.io/jam/h3dgamejam2/entries). Solve puzzles to help slimes make their way through a winter wonderland! Do you have what it takes to be the greatest of all slime!?
